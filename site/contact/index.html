What should I prepare for the intro call?

Nothing formal. It helps if you can share what your product does, which markets you are targeting, your regulatory status (for example MiCA authorization or plans for it) and what you have already tried in DACH. We take it from there.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, if you need one before sharing details about your launch, just mention it in your message and we will handle it before the call.

Which languages do you work in?

We work in English and German. All German-facing content – PR, creator briefings, website copy, community – is written natively in German.
