Partnerships

Why partnerships carry extra weight in German crypto

Crypto partnerships in Germany put your product where German-speaking users already research and compare: comparison platforms, crypto portals, trusted creators, exchanges, wallets and fintechs. In a market with a high trust threshold, a recommendation from an established local name often counts for more than any ad you could run.

Paid reach is limited anyway. Major ad platforms restrict crypto advertising and require authorization in many EU markets, which makes partners one of the few channels that scale. Comparison sites and portals also deliver what SEO needs: backlinks and discovery from sites search engines already trust. Affiliate and co-marketing deals follow the same rules as your own marketing – labelled, fair, clear and not misleading.

We build these partnerships hands-on: identifying the partners that matter in your category, negotiating listings, affiliate terms and co-marketing, and planning event presence – our founder co-founded Germany's largest blockchain conference. Partnerships work best alongside PR and SEO content. Once Germany works, our network in France, Spain and Italy helps you take the next step. Our DACH market comparison shows how Germany, Austria and Switzerland differ.

Which partners matter most in the German crypto market?

It depends on your product. For consumer products, the final decision often happens on comparison platforms and crypto portals; for infrastructure or B2B products, co-marketing with exchanges, wallets and fintechs usually matters more. Creators, newsletters and events complete the picture. We map the options for your category and prioritize by audience fit, credibility and SEO value.

How does crypto affiliate marketing work under MiCA and German advertising rules?

Affiliate content is marketing, so the same rules apply as for your own channels: it must be recognizable as advertising, and claims about crypto-assets must be fair, clear and not misleading and, where a white paper is required, consistent with it. We give affiliates clear rules on disclosure and approved claims, agreed with your legal counsel. This is not legal advice.

Can partnerships help our SEO?

Yes, when partners are chosen for relevance. Listings and articles on established German crypto portals and comparison sites bring backlinks, referral traffic and brand searches – signals that support your rankings. For Waltio, partnerships with sites such as Bitcoin2Go and Bitcoin-Kurier added visibility, discovery and backlinks alongside the SEO foundation. We stay away from low-quality link schemes that put your domain at risk.

Are crypto events in Germany worth it?

Selectively, yes. Events are valuable for partnerships, media contacts and community, but expensive when they are not tied to clear goals. We help you choose the right DACH events, prepare speaker applications, side events and meeting plans and handle the follow-up. Our founder co-founded Germany's largest blockchain conference, so we know what organizers and attendees expect.

Can we expand to France, Spain or Italy afterwards?

Yes – that is the scale step in our Test → Win → Scale approach. Once Germany has shown which partners and messages work, our network in France, Spain and Italy lets us launch campaigns there without starting from zero. Germany is a demanding test market: a setup that works here is a strong basis for the rest of Europe.
