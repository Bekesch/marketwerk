PR & Media

What crypto PR in Germany has to deliver

A crypto PR agency for Germany gets your brand into the media German-speaking users trust – and makes sure what appears there is accurate, compliant and worth reading. That takes press releases written in German, story angles with local relevance and direct pitching to editors in crypto, finance and tech.

The media landscape is broader than crypto news. Specialist outlets such as BTC-ECHO and Cointelegraph Deutsch sit next to business and finance media like Handelsblatt and finanzen.net, tech media like t3n, Der Brutkasten and Trending Topics in Austria and finews.ch in Switzerland. Each has its own audience and its own idea of news. A translated global press release rarely makes it into any of them.

German editors look for substance: what changes for users in Germany, how your product handles security and regulation, and why the story matters now. We build PR around those questions, time it around moments like MiCA milestones or tax season and connect it with KOL campaigns and SEO content, so coverage keeps working after the news cycle. Our crypto PR guide for Germany covers the media side in detail.

Which media do you target in Germany, Austria and Switzerland?

It depends on the story and the audience. Typical targets include crypto media such as BTC-ECHO and Cointelegraph Deutsch, business and finance media such as Handelsblatt and finanzen.net, tech media such as t3n, Der Brutkasten and Trending Topics in Austria and finews.ch in Switzerland. We pitch every outlet individually and don't claim exclusive media partnerships with any of them.

Do you promise a fixed number of articles?

No. Earned coverage depends on editorial decisions, so a fixed number of articles can't honestly be promised. We commit to the work: strong angles, native German materials and persistent, targeted pitching. Where reach has to be predictable, we recommend sponsored placements – always clearly labelled as advertising, as German media law and MiCA's rules on marketing communications require.

Do you write press releases in German?

Yes – natively, not as translations. German editors notice translated copy immediately: awkward phrasing, wrong financial terms and angles that only matter outside Germany. We write releases, quotes and boilerplates in German from the start, adapt them for Austria and Switzerland where needed and can provide an English version for internal approval.

How does MiCA affect crypto PR?

MiCA requires marketing communications about crypto-assets to be clearly identifiable as marketing, fair, clear and not misleading, and consistent with the white paper where one is required. Editorial coverage is the journalist's responsibility, but your press releases, quotes and sponsored content are your communication. We align claims with your legal counsel. Our MiCA marketing guide explains the rules. This is not legal advice.

Do you work with token projects, exchanges or DeFi protocols?

We work with crypto brands such as Waltio, ether.fi, Blever, Nest Collective and Anon Summit, and we decide on new projects case by case. What matters is a product we can credibly explain to German media and users, and a regulatory position your counsel has reviewed. We don't promote projects that rely on price promises.
